Getting Around

Taniti City is flat and easy to walk around. Merriton Landing is also easy to walk.

Buses

Public buses run in Taniti City every day from 5am to 11pm. There are private buses for the rest of the island.

Taxis

You can get a taxi in Taniti City.

Rental Cars

There is a car rental place near the airport.

Bikes

A few shops rent bikes. You have to wear a helmet, it's the law.

Getting to Taniti

Most people fly into the airport. There is also a small cruise ship that stops in Yellow Leaf Bay one night a week.
